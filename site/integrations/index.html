Live integrations

Eight core systems, all in production.

AT

Autotask PSA

Read tickets · Write back on dispatcher click

The core integration. Nova polls Autotask continuously — fast during business hours, quieter overnight — for new and updated tickets, classifies them, and writes the dispatcher's accepted classification back to the ticket — priority, queue, type, issue and category. No custom fields. No PSA add-ons.

Microsoft Entra ID

SSO + role-based permissions

Single sign-on for Nova staff and the client portal. Staff sign in with their Microsoft account, under your existing sign-in policies. Access is set by role inside Nova — for example, dispatcher or tech.

Anthropic Claude

AI triage, Pulse and summaries

Anthropic powers Nova's classification, briefings, and end-of-day reports out of the box. You bring your own API key, billed directly by Anthropic. Anthropic's commercial terms don't use your API data to train models.

BYO keyNo training on your dataDefault provider
OA

OpenAI

Configurable per-process fallback

For MSPs with existing OpenAI relationships or compliance constraints. Any AI process (triage, briefings, summaries) can run on OpenAI instead of Anthropic. Same prompts, your choice of provider.

BYO keyPer-process toggle

GoTo Connect

Phone system · live call state · call linking

Inbound and outbound calls auto-matched to Autotask companies and contacts. Live call state flows into the Status Board so dispatch sees who a tech is on the phone with, in real time. Call recordings + AI-generated summaries sync to Nova; matching tickets are proposed automatically with one-click linking. Read the Call Linking deep dive →
