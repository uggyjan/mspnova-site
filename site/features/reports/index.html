/ Client Reviews

A monthly review for every client, written for you.

Each month Nova drafts a short service review for every contracted client — tickets, response, satisfaction and what stood out — with a plain-language summary written by AI. You can preview and refresh it, and clients can open it in their portal.

  • Built from data Nova already keeps — no extra Autotask work
  • AI-written summary in plain language
  • Clients open it in the portal
  • Preview and refresh before clients see it

Who reads it

  • Service manager
    The scorecard, SLA and CSAT, week by week.
  • Owner
    Finance and account views across the client base.
  • Your clients
    Their own monthly review and reports, in the portal.
