Inside the portal

Six surfaces. Everything clients usually call you for.

Ticket history

Open + closed tickets, filtered to the user or their org. Status, owner, last update, public comments only.

New ticket form

Clients describe the problem in plain words. The ticket lands in Autotask and Nova triages it like any other.

Reports

Monthly reports with charts, plus an AI-written monthly review of their service.

CSAT widget

Per-ticket satisfaction survey, with results feeding your CSAT reports.

Assets

Managers see their devices, who has them, and their history. Turned on per client.

Invoices

Billing history and payment through ConnectBooster, signed in with the same account.

/ White-label

Your logo. Your colors. Your domain.

The portal lives at portal.yourmsp.com (or any subdomain you choose). Your wordmark in the header, your brand colors throughout, your support contact info in the footer. Clients never see "MSPNova" — they see you.

  • Custom domain with managed SSL
  • Logo upload, primary + accent color tokens
  • Header / footer text fully editable
/ Authentication

SSO that actually works on day one.

Most clients use Microsoft 365. The portal supports M365 SSO out of the box — clients click "Sign in with Microsoft," get redirected, return authenticated. Google sign-in is supported too, and email sign-in covers clients without either.

  • Microsoft 365 SSO (Entra ID)
  • Google Workspace SSO
  • Email sign-in for everyone else
  • Roles — for example, managers see their whole company
