/ Mobile check-in

Tap on arrival. Tap on departure. Dispatch sees it.

Techs open Nova on their phone, see today's on-site visits, and tap "Check in" when they arrive. The status updates in dispatch right away, and the visit is recorded against their day.

  • Geofenced check-in confirms arrival at client site
  • Visits feed the time clock and timesheets
  • Travel time and mileage logged
/ Tech Status Board

Field check-in flips a card on the dispatch board.

When a tech taps "Check in" on-site, their card on the Tech Status Board flips to "On Site" with the client name and a duration timer. Dispatch sees who's where, who's running long, and who's free for the next escalation — all without anyone touching their status.

  • On-Site state with client name + arrival timer
  • Long visits are flagged to dispatch
  • One-click Ping from dispatch sends a Teams DM
