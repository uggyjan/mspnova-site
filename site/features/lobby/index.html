/ Setup

A new TV in a few minutes.

An admin creates a display, drags panels onto a grid in the layout builder, and previews it before saving. On the TV, open the Lobby page in its browser and enter the display's 6-digit PIN. That's it — the screen stays signed in to that one display and nothing else.

  • Drag-and-drop layout builder with preview
  • Save a layout as a template and reuse it on other TVs
  • Each TV has its own layout, refresh rate and theme
  • Reset a screen's PIN or disable it at any time, and see when each one last checked in

Where it fits

Different from the Tech Status Board: the board is the dispatcher's working screen; Lobby is for the room.

  • Service desk
    Queue health and response times where everyone can see them.
  • Reception or front office
    Client feedback, wins and who's on — the good news, on show.
  • Break room
    Celebrations, top closer and the weather.
