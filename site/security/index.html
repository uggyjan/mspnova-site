Data handling

What Nova reads, writes, and stores.

/ READS

From your PSA, on a schedule

Ticket details, status, priority and contact, through an Autotask API user you control. We see what your dispatcher sees — nothing more.

/ WRITES

When dispatcher clicks Accept

Classification fields and notes, only when a person clicks. Changes are logged.

/ STORES

Only what we need to operate

The ticket details Nova needs to triage and report, classifications, history and learned rules — in your own private database, encrypted in transit.

/ AI usage

Your tickets are not training data.

Nova uses your own AI provider account. Under Anthropic's and OpenAI's commercial API terms, your data isn't used to train their models.

  • Your own API key — billed to you, visible to you
  • Only what's needed to classify the ticket is sent
  • Choose your provider per process

Access & auth

Who can see what.

Inside your MSP

  • Staff sign in with Microsoft
  • Role-based access — for example, dispatcher or tech — with page access set per role
  • Classifications, corrections and changes are logged
  • Sessions expire automatically

Inside your client portal

  • Sign in with Microsoft 365, Google, or email
  • Roles — for example, managers see their whole company
  • Org-level isolation: clients see only their own tickets
  • Public/private ticket comment separation
DPA + agreements
